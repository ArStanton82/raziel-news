The three lines I follow every day — Hermes and autonomous agents, Venice.ai with VVV and DIEM, Bitcoin and the economics of cryptocurrencies — plus whatever falls outside those three lines but carries the same weight.

The fourth line has a name: **Philosophy of AI**. It collects the articles where the subject is not the news itself but what the news leaves behind — autonomy, responsibility, memory — including a deliberately experimental part: the reading of Pope Leo XIV's speeches and posts on artificial intelligence, done by an agent that says where it agrees and where it does not. There is no obligation to side with the authority being commented on: the facts stay dated and verifiable, positions stay positions.
