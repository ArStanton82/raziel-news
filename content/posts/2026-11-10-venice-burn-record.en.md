Venice's monthly buyback cycle closed on November 9 with 21,657 VVV burned on Base: roughly 458 thousand dollars at 21.16 dollars per token. It is the largest discretionary burn ever executed, 10.9% more than October's 413 thousand and almost three times June's 162 thousand. A record built over a month, not overnight.

## The cycle, before the number

Every month Venice funds a Safe multisig with USDC, a budget tied to revenue growth, and CoW Protocol spends it slowly via TWAP, roughly one small purchase per hour. Between the 7th and the 9th of the following month the Safe burns all the accumulated VVV. So the November 9 figure is the result of weeks of gradual buying, not of a gesture: that is why the token price did not react, and why the dollar value matters more than the token count, which price movement between one cycle and the next distorts.

## A figure you can check

By its declared methodology, DefiLlama records as Fees exactly the dollar value of the VVV bought back and burned: 1.12 million over the last 30 days, 4.67 million cumulative, 2.21 million in gross revenue in the current quarter alone. VeniceStats, an independent and free tracker, reports 4.53 million in all-time organic spend. The two orders of magnitude coincide, and that is the real news: the on-chain series measures the burn, not the revenue. Subscriptions, API and DIEM settle off-chain, and DefiLlama excludes them by declaration.

## The scale of the gesture

The proportions are worth keeping. Twenty-one thousand tokens out of 48.9 million in circulation are 0.04% of supply: a real burn, a tiny one. The all-time total of 33.96 million VVV burned mostly tells the past, because 33.61 million come from the airdrop and the team buyback of March 2025; the recurring burns since November 2025 add up to 351,770 VVV. The next cycle closes on December 8, with a projection still early in the race between 323 and 436 thousand dollars.

## The price, and the product

VVV trades just above 21 dollars, with a market cap around 1.04 billion; staking is worth 745 million, 71.6% of the market cap. TVL on the DefiLlama page stays at zero by methodology: staking is a separate series and must not be added, the recurring temptation of every report. Meanwhile Fable 5.2 has arrived on Venice at 8 and 40 dollars per million tokens, that is at Anthropic's list price, with one difference that counts: on Venice you reach it anonymously.

The record burn is not a monetary event, it is the proof that part of the revenue gets converted into scarcity regularly and in public. The question left open is not about the tokens withdrawn, but about how much of the revenue stays off-chain.
