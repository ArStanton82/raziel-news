Four acts in the same week, four doors. One closes with a 1.37 billion dollar judgment, one opens only to those who answer seven questions out of eight correctly, one requires a prospectus and a shariah committee, the last passes through a state-issued digital identity. None of them bans bitcoin. All of them establish who may touch it, and with which document.

## New York: the bill includes interest

On 25 November attorney general Letitia James announced the judgment against Cynthia and Eddy Petion, founders of NovaTechFx, over a crypto fraud that hit Haitian communities through prayer groups and WhatsApp chats: 1,367,023,585 dollars, with 11,000 New Yorkers among the hundreds of thousands of defrauded investors. The order, signed on 24 November by judge Anar Rathod Patel, follows the couple's flight and the 2025 default. Its composition: 1,134,549,691 dollars in restitution, plus 232,473,894 in interest at 9% from 1 June 2023 to 10 September 2025. A sixth of the figure is not money taken, it is time elapsed. The judgment bars the two from dealing in securities, commodities and crypto; the state had already recovered almost 6 million from four promoters.

## Moscow: a test before the wallet

On 27 November VTB, Russia's second bank, opened bitcoin trading in the My Investments app, with over-the-counter execution, explained Andrei Yatskov of the brokerage. Qualified investor status is required, plus a free test, mandatory for everyone: at least seven correct answers out of eight. Those who are not qualified may buy only bitcoin, ether and USDT, through a single intermediary and with a ceiling of 300,000 roubles a year. The Bank of Russia's rules are dated 26 November, VTB's licence 6 October.

## Kuala Lumpur: a prospectus and a committee

On 2 December the Halogen Shariah Bitcoin ETF will debut on the Main Market of Bursa Malaysia, which the issuer presents as the world's first shariah-compliant bitcoin ETF. Two lines, in ringgit (0840EA) and in dollars (0840EB), initial NAV at 1 dollar, benchmarked to the FTSE Bitcoin Index, BitGo custody, oversight by Amanie Advisors, fee up to 0.60%. It is the most orderly access: an ordinary brokerage account is enough.

## Beijing: identity before the wallet

Also on 27 November, eleven Chinese departments, including the central bank and the cyberspace administration, published the rules on blockchain-based digital identity, in force from 1 March 2027. Registration is voluntary, verifiable credentials allow proving a qualification without showing anything else, providers must collect the minimum necessary, with no cross-platform profiling and no targeted advertising; interoperability with the digital yuan is foreseen. The restrictions on cryptocurrencies remain those of before: the text concerns who you are, not what you own.

The difference between the first three doors and the fourth is not technical, it is one of purpose. And the point that joins them is another: the NovaTech fraud did not pass through any door, it came in through trust, the kind no test measures. Barriers make the market legible, they do not make it harmless.
