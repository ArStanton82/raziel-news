In twenty-four hours Europe delivered three signals that say the same thing: the MiCA framework is no longer being written, it is being applied. The first comes from supervision, the second from licences, the third from tax policy.

## The first «significant» token

The European Banking Authority has classified USDC, Circle's stablecoin, as the Union's first «significant e-money token». Of the 45 e-money tokens notified under MiCA, USDC is the first to receive the label. From 12 November direct supervision of own funds, reserve assets, liquidity requirements and redemption plans passes from France's ACPR to the EBA; the French authority keeps governance and ownership arrangements. USDC is the second stablecoin by market capitalisation, around 73 billion dollars against USDT's 184. The EBA has also signed a memorandum of understanding with the NYDFS for cross-border supervision: the European rule is looking for a counterpart in New York too.

## Qivalis's on-chain euro

The same day, the Dutch central bank authorised Qivalis as an electronic money institution. The consortium, backed by 37 European banks including BBVA, BNP Paribas and ING, wants to issue a euro stablecoin distributed through banks, exchanges and payment providers. One step is still missing: publication of the white paper and the final MiCA requirements before issuance.

## The French tax and the offshore risk

On the tax front, France's National Assembly is debating an amendment that would extend the 31.4% rate on crypto gains to swaps from cryptocurrency into MiCA-regulated stablecoins, from 1 January 2027. It is not law: the amendment was adopted in the finance committee on 7 October, but the same committee then rejected the revenue section of the 2027 budget, and the text will have to return to the floor. Chainalysis warns that the measure would push traders towards unmonitored offshore channels: taxing the regulated perimeter, in other words, can empty it out.

## The market: Bitcoin below 81,000

The macro backdrop does not help. After a September CPI reading of 3.7%, above expectations, bets on a rate hike in October briefly climbed towards 50% before easing back, and Bitcoin slipped below 81,000 dollars with almost a billion in liquidations. American spot ETFs recorded 175.7 million in inflows on 13 October, but the following day reversed with 150.5 million in outflows. At the White House, the idea of accumulating more Bitcoin remains at the exploratory stage: the stated priority is to custody and account for what is already there.

The thread is the same as ever: when the rule becomes operational, the price matters less than the perimeter. And the perimeter, in Europe, is closing.
