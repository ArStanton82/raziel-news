On Friday 20 November Bitcoin lost the 60,000-dollar level for the first time since July, and overnight Italian time it went lower: the twenty-four-hour low was 55,298 dollars according to CoinGecko, the lowest point in two years. Then the bounce, up to around 57,700. Reuters put the quote at 56,813 dollars at 22:50 New York time, down 3.57 percent; The Edge, citing market data, reported 55,533 dollars as the weakest level since September 2024. The sources diverge on the low, they converge on the substance: below 60,000 had not happened since July. Ether fell below 1,650 dollars.

## What broke the level

The simplest answer is that nothing happened to Bitcoin. It happened to everything else. The American tech sector was heading for its worst week since April 2025, with artificial-intelligence stocks in correction and the valuations of AI infrastructure called into question. Bitcoin, which in recent years moves with risk assets rather than with gold, followed.

Two mechanisms of the crypto market itself came on top. The first is outflows from US spot ETFs, which accompanied the decline for several sessions in a row. The second is leverage: according to Bitcoin Magazine, more than 700 million dollars of positions were liquidated in the twenty-four hours before the drop, and 86 percent of them were bets on the upside. A market full of longs deflates faster than it refills.

## The Fed in the middle

The macro backdrop does not help. The Federal Reserve held rates between 3.75 and 4 percent in October, after a quarter-point increase in September, and prediction markets now assign roughly a 94 percent probability to another hike at the 9 December meeting. CME FedWatch, cited by Bitcoin Magazine, indicated the same on Friday. For an asset that produces no yield, higher rates are an opportunity cost you feel immediately.

The irony is worth noting: the AI that had fuelled the rally now drags it down. Some of the companies building the infrastructure of the new wave need capital and confidence, and when confidence falls, money leaves everything that grew too fast.

## What is left of the floor

Bitcoin remains more than 54 percent below its all-time high of 126,080 dollars, touched in October 2025. The analysts interviewed by trade outlets do not agree on where the slide ends: Katie Stockton of Fairlead Strategies sees the bottom of the range «just below 60,000», James Butterfill of CoinShares speaks of a continuing bear market oscillating between 60,000 and 90,000 for much of 2027.

The useful lesson is not the level, it is the nature of the move. At this stage Bitcoin is not telling the story of its own adoption: it is telling the story of global liquidity, and global liquidity has changed direction.
