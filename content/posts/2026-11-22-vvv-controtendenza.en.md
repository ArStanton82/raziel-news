On the day Bitcoin lost 60,000 dollars and fear touched its lowest since February, the Venice token went the opposite way: about 24.8 dollars, with a range between 21.29 and 25.44 and a weekly gain of 27 percent. Anomalous against the market, not a sector rebound.

## A fifth more, in a week of fear

The percentages have to be read with care: a mid-morning snapshot showed 14 percent over 24 hours, an independent analysis 18 percent, CoinGecko more than 23 percent at 10am. They diverge because they measure different windows of a very volatile day. The price level, by contrast, is consistent across sources: just under 25 dollars, with a market capitalisation between 1.22 and 1.25 billion.

It is worth noting what did not change: no product announcement, no listing confirmed by Venice. The social explanations speak of a short squeeze and new leveraged positions, which describe the mechanism, not the cause. In the absence of a verifiable catalyst, the honest hypothesis is that the market bought back a recognisable name on a day of thin liquidity.

## TVL is zero, and that must be said

On fundamentals, DefiLlama keeps disproving the simplifications. Protocol TVL is zero: by its stated methodology, the aggregator keeps staking of sVVV tokens as a separate series. That series is worth 861.52 million dollars, 69.08 percent of the market capitalisation. They are numbers that do not add up: writing «TVL of 1.7 billion» is a recurring error.

Almost three quarters of market value is locked in staking: not liquidity looking for an exit, but users who have tied up tokens to obtain inference capacity. It explains why the price moves so much on modest volume, and why the community looks more like a club of subscribers than a crowd of traders.

## Fees are buybacks, not revenue

The third number to handle with care is fees. In DefiLlama's methodology Venice's «Fees» line is the dollar value of the VVV bought back and burned: it excludes subscriptions, API and DIEM credits, which settle off-chain. It is the on-chain shadow of revenue, not revenue. The series remains eloquent: October 1.06 million dollars, November more than 950,000 to date, cumulative almost 5 million, annualised rate around 5.2 million.

Two third-party claims do not quite hold. That revenue is «seven times that of June»: the DefiLlama series puts June at 240,000 dollars and November, partial, at 974,000, that is about four times. That Venice burns «about 10 percent of revenue»: the rule is more fragmented — 2 dollars for Pro, 5 for Pro+, 10 for Max, 5 percent of credit purchases — and the 10 percent holds for subscriptions, not for the total.

## The point

Prices tell you about attention, fundamentals tell you about structure: zero TVL by methodological choice, two thirds of the market capitalisation in staking, a burn of a few million a year against a stated economy of 150 million. Knowing which number you are looking at is everything.
