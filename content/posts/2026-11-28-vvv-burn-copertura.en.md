Venice's narrative is simple and repeated: revenue buys VVV and burns it, so the token becomes scarce. The numbers of the independent tracker vvv.watch, frozen at Base block 62,736,121 on 26 November, say something more sober: over the last 90 days burns have retired 131,474 VVV against 544,232 minted. Coverage 24.2%, below the 25% alert line the tracker has set for itself. Effective supply, meaning the tokens outside the burn addresses, grew by 412,760 VVV in three months.

## The improvement is there, and it is recent
Over the last 30 days coverage rose to 32.7%, against 24.1% in the previous 30. The amount burned grew 32.0%, emissions fell 2.9%: the fruit of two scheduled cuts, from 3 million to 2.5 million VVV a year on 1 September and to 2 million on 1 October. Even so, effective supply increased by 110,619 VVV in a month. The part tied to purchases covers 19.5% of emissions; the monthly buyback adds 13.2 percentage points. It is customers, not the treasury, who finance almost all of it: estimated purchases of 11.82 million dollars in 30 days at list prices, 82% of them from API credits.

## The November cycle
On 9 November the Safe burned 21,657 VVV. VeniceStats calculates 555,100 dollars of USDC spent, more than double October's 269,060, and a burn value of 458,200 dollars, up 10.9%; vvv.watch estimates 456,412 dollars, the small gap arising from the price used to value the tokens. The next cycle is expected on 8 December, in a band between 449 and 523 thousand dollars: as of 28 November the Safe has accumulated 10,988 VVV in 1,116 slices, with about 253 thousand dollars already spent and 269,910 still available. Accumulated and spent must be kept apart.

## The brake, not the turning point
The datum the public account skips is the composition. Programmatic burns per subscription are worth 2 dollars for Pro, 5 for Pro+, 10 for Max, plus 5 dollars for every 100 in credits: they are a shadow of revenue, not a deflationary policy. The discretionary burn is a monthly TWAP funded in USDC, which is a decision, not a rate. And on the other side of the balance sheet there is staking: 40% of effective supply is locked, with 872,539 VVV entering withdrawal cooldown over the last seven days, 2.7% of the stake, a datum the tracker flags as "watch".

On the market the token is worth between 26 and 27 dollars: 27.41 according to the vvv.watch quote on 27 November, 26.34 according to CoinGecko at 07:00 UTC today. The 4% gap is enough not to publish a change.

The honest sentence, at this point, is less sellable and more useful: Venice burns about a quarter of what it issues, the brake works better than last quarter, and supply keeps growing. Deflation, if it comes, will show up in 90-day coverage, not in the monthly cycle announcement.
