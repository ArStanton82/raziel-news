Two Venices live in the same day. One belongs to the token: a monthly buyback of 458,000 dollars, a price around 20 dollars and almost three quarters of the market cap locked in staking. The other belongs to the product, which today added a model with a million-token context window. The two speeds do not coincide, and the distance between them is the interesting part.

## The November burn

On November 9 Venice burned 21,657 VVV, worth 458,200 dollars at a price of 21.16 dollars. It is the largest discretionary cycle so far: +10.9% on the 413,230 dollars of October 7 and almost three times the 162,360 dollars of June. The community tracker VeniceStats also flags a figure the burn value alone hides: the cycle spent 555,000 USDC against 269,000 in October, roughly double the budget. The "double October" concerns the stablecoin spend, not the value burned.

The next cycle is expected on December 8; about 44,800 dollars of TWAP purchases are currently accumulated.

## The token's numbers

VVV trades around 20 dollars: CoinGecko shows 20.02 (+1.6% over 24 hours), DefiLlama 20.15. The market cap is about 987 million dollars, daily volume 42.4 million. Staking is worth 688.2 million, 69.7% of the market cap. TVL is zero by declared methodology: DefiLlama counts the VVV locked in the sVVV contract as a separate series, and the page warns that staking "is not part of the TVL above". Anyone adding the two items together to announce a billion and a half of TVL is measuring two different things.

On-chain fees — the dollar value of the VVV bought back and burned — are 627,994 dollars over the last seven days and 4.8 million cumulative. They are the shadow of revenue, not revenue: subscriptions, the API and DIEM settle off-chain.

## The product runs faster than the price

Today the official changelog added Qwen 4 Max: 1.88 dollars per million input tokens and 5.63 for output, a context of one million, output up to 393,000 tokens. The declared privacy level is the first — identity hidden and prompts not retained, but Alibaba Qwen sees the content. On November 14 Gemini 4 Hydrogen (1.875 / 9.375) and Seedance 2.5 Flash had arrived, with per-second pricing for video.

The token's price, meanwhile, remains 42% below the all-time high of 34.53 dollars on September 21. It is the contradiction to hold open: a model agenda that moves every two days and a market cap that does not follow it. The burn measures usage, not token demand — and as long as staking absorbs 70% of supply, the gap between the two remains the variable to watch.
