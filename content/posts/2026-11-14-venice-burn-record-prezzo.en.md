There is a contradiction worth looking in the face. On November 9 Venice burned 21,657 VVV on Base, the largest discretionary burn in its history: 458,200 dollars at a price of 21.16 dollars per token, 10.9 per cent more than the October cycle (413,230 dollars). Over the same period the token kept falling. The deflationary machine works; the market, for now, does not follow it.

## A series that has been rising for six cycles

The figure is not isolated: June 162,360 dollars, July 266,050, August 318,640, September 379,970, October 413,230, November 458,200. Every cycle bigger than the one before. The all-time burned total has reached 33.96 million VVV.

The components must be kept apart. The November 9 burn is the discretionary part: a USDC budget paid each month into a Safe and spent through a TWAP, a small purchase every hour, until the accumulated tokens are destroyed. The rest is programmatic burn, tied to subscriptions (2 dollars for Pro, 5 for Pro+, 10 for Max) and to credits (5 dollars every 100). Calling the entire monthly amount a "voluntary buyback" is a mistake that circulates often.

## The buyback is the shadow of revenue, not revenue

On DefiLlama, Venice's "Fees" line, by its own declared methodology, is the dollar value of the VVV bought back and burned: 1.13 million over the last 30 days, 24,123 dollars in the last 24 hours, 4.76 million cumulated. They only capture what passes on-chain: subscriptions, API and DIEM settle off-chain and never appear. The burn is the shadow of revenue, not revenue.

In the same hours @venicestats claims that revenue and burn are at all-time highs on the same day, while VVV is living "its worst week in a while". Without figures the claim cannot be verified: it is a statement from an interested party.

## Zero TVL, over 70 per cent of market cap in staking

The picture on November 14 is this: TVL of zero dollars, because DefiLlama counts staking as a separate series, "not part of TVL". That series is worth 638.06 million, 71.66 per cent of the 890.46 million market cap. The price is around 18 dollars, almost 49 per cent below the 34.53 peak of September 21; on a weekly basis CoinGecko shows minus 13.2 per cent, DefiLlama minus 9.7. With a gap that wide we do not publish the change: the level is what counts, at half the peak.

## The product runs ahead of the token

Meanwhile the catalog keeps moving: on November 12 Pareto (one-million-token window, 1 dollar input and 4 output) and Gemini 4 Carbon (2.50 and 12.50); on November 14 Gemini 4 Hydrogen (1.875 and 9.375). Aggressive prices, anonymous access: that is the part of the company growing without asking the chart for permission. A record burn worth one eighth of the monthly issuance is not enough to hold up a valuation, but it says the cash flow exists. The real question is not whether Venice burns enough, but whether the market will sooner or later look at revenue instead of price.
