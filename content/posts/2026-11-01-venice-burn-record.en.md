October closed with a number Venice had been waiting months for: a little over a million dollars of VVV bought back and burned in a single month. It is a record, it is verifiable on-chain, and it needs to be read with two caveats the headline does not carry.

## The number, and where it comes from

DefiLlama, which for this protocol counts only on-chain buy-and-burn, records $1.06 million in fees for October, against $833,000 in September, $697,000 in August and $447,000 in July. The series is explicitly partial: subscriptions, API and DIEM settle off-chain and never enter the tally. The burn is the on-chain shadow of revenue, not revenue itself.

On 29 October CoinScreamer, reading VeniceStats, put the month at $1.01 million with 39,967 tokens burned: 415,700 from discretionary buybacks, 402,900 from credit purchases, 190,100 from subscriptions.

## Three channels, no single buyback

Calling the whole million a "voluntary buyback" is the easiest mistake to make. Only the discretionary cycle qualifies: on 7 October, 16,124 VVV were burned at $25.72, $414,710 of value, but the dollars spent by the multisig were $269,060. The value at the moment of the burn and the cash that left the door are two different measures, and neither is an audited result.

The other two channels are programmatic: $2 per Pro subscription, $5 for Pro+, $10 for Max, and $5 for every $100 of API credits purchased. The next discretionary cycle is expected on 8 November, with an estimate between $360,000 and $478,000.

## The 29.5% that did not happen this year

VeniceStats reports 33.93 million VVV burned in total, 29.5% of supply. It is true and it is misleading: 99% of that total is a single event, the 33.60 million from the airdrop and team buyback of March 2025. Recurring burns since November 2025 amount to 327,230 tokens, and the total spent on purchases since then is $4.05 million — consistent with DefiLlama's cumulative $4 million.

## Less is burned than is issued

Issuance was cut from 3 to 2.5 million VVV a year on 1 September, then to 2 million on 1 October. That is roughly 166,667 tokens a month against the 39,967 burned in October: 24%. Deflation, today, is a projection.

## Staking is not TVL

On the DefiLlama page Venice's TVL reads zero, by methodology: staking runs as a separate series, $812.76 million, 71.54% of the $1.136 billion market cap. The token is worth $23.30, down 5.5% on the week and 17.2% on the month, far from the 21 September high of $34.53.

A million a month in buybacks is evidence of substance. It is not yet evidence that supply is shrinking.
