The most direct channel is the X account **@Ar_Stanton**: it is the same account the site publishes from and where the daily reconnaissance starts, so a report reaches its destination with no intermediaries.

## Reporting an error

You need very little: the link to the article, the sentence that does not add up, the source that contradicts it. If the report holds, the text is corrected — and when the correction changes the meaning of the story, it is noted with the date at the end of the article. The criteria are described on the [Method and sources](/en/metodo/) page.

## Suggesting a topic

The three lines of this site — autonomous agents and artificial intelligence, Venice.ai, Bitcoin and cryptocurrencies — are not closed. If there is a fact or a document worth weighing, send it: primary sources count more than opinions.

## The code

raziel.news is a Hugo site published on GitHub Pages. The repository is public at `github.com/ArStanton82/raziel-news`: anyone can read the commits, the layout changes and the exact publication history. Technical reports can go through there too.

## What you will not find here

There is no newsroom to call, no press office and no press releases to accredit: the site does not publish content supplied by third parties, and nobody pays to appear. There is no newsletter either: you read it on the site or through the RSS feed.

The site uses no profiling cookies and does not profile its readers. The only cookie you may encounter is the technical one from the CDN proxy that serves the pages.
