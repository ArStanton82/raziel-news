MoonPay seeks US bank charter for stablecoin, custody services

Latest NewsPublishedNov 19, 2026

MoonPay’s proposed national trust bank would offer stablecoin issuance and redemption, reserve management and digital asset custody under federal oversight.

Crypto payments firm MoonPay has applied for a US national trust bank charter to offer stablecoin issuance and redemption, reserve management and digital asset custody services.

According to an OCC filing, MoonPay submitted its application on Tuesday to establish MoonPay National Trust Bank, a non-depository institution headquartered in Miami Beach, Florida. The proposed bank would not accept traditional deposits or issue loans.

MoonPay already provides stablecoin issuance infrastructure to businesses, including support for branded digital dollars. If approved, the charter would allow its proposed banking subsidiary to provide stablecoin and custody services under federal supervision.

The application comes as US regulators prepare to implement the GENIUS Act, which takes effect on Jan. 18, 2027, and establishes federal standards for stablecoin issuers, including reserve backing and redemption requirements.

With the filing, MoonPay joins several crypto and payments companies seeking federal trust bank charters, including Rain, Modern Treasury and Block. Coinbase’s charter took effect earlier this month, while Circle received final OCC approval in July.

The rise in crypto firms applying for federal trust bank charters has drawn opposition from community banks. In October, the Independent Community Bankers of America sued the OCC, arguing that the regulator exceeded its authority by allowing non-depository trust banks to conduct activities traditionally associated with commercial banks.
