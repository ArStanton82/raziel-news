Crypto News Today: Bitcoin MVRV Near 1.1 Signals Possible Bottom as ETH and XRP Join Market Recovery

Ahmed Ishtiaque17 Nov 2026
Assets: BTC ETH XRP
Bitcoin (BTC), Crypto Market, Crypto News, Ethereum (ETH), XRP

Bitcoin's valuation metrics are flashing a possible bottom signal, but persistent ETF withdrawals and weak on-chain demand continue to complicate the outlook for BTC, ETH, and XRP.

Bitcoin (BTC) traded at $64,946.14 after recovering from an early session low near $64,357. Ethereum (ETH) and XRP each gained 1.04%, reaching $1,838.34 and $0.9646, respectively. The rebound follows a sharp market correction that pushed Bitcoin toward $62,000 over the weekend.

The latest developments have placed Bitcoin’s MVRV ratio, short-term holder cost basis, and institutional investment flows at the center of market discussions. While some on-chain indicators suggest BTC is approaching historically undervalued territory, declining apparent demand raises questions about whether buyers can sustain a recovery. Current asset prices and broader market movements can be tracked through Brave New Coin’s Bitcoin price chart and cryptocurrency market dashboard.

Bitcoin MVRV Near 1.1 Revives Historical Market Bottom Signals

CryptoQuant’s latest analysis shows Bitcoin’s Market Value to Realized Value (MVRV) ratio falling below 1.2, with its current reading hovering around 1.1. The indicator compares Bitcoin’s market capitalization with its realized capitalization, which estimates the value of circulating coins based on the prices at which they last moved on-chain.

Bitcoin’s MVRV ratio near 1.1 at $64,100 mirrors the undervaluation levels seen around the 2022 market bottom before a significant recovery. Source: CryptoQuant via X

A declining MVRV ratio generally indicates that the market’s aggregate valuation is approaching the average cost basis of its holders. Historically, readings below 1.2 have appeared during periods of substantial market stress, when unrealized profits across the network have contracted, and Bitcoin has approached potential accumulation zones.

CryptoQuant analyst Darkfost highlighted this historical relationship, describing the sub-1.2 reading as a signal of “a forming market bottom.” The analyst also noted that the indicator had reached similar levels during the 2022 bear market, when Bitcoin traded between approximately $16,000 and $21,000 before beginning its subsequent recovery.

The current reading represents the third time the signal has appeared during the 2026 bear market. Previous occurrences emerged in February, when BTC approached $60,000, and again during June and July, when the cryptocurrency declined toward $57,000.

However, historical similarities do not establish that another bottom has already formed. MVRV measures market valuation relative to on-chain cost basis, rather than identifying fresh buying activity. A low reading can therefore persist while prices remain under pressure, particularly when investors continue reducing their exposure.

Bitcoin Tests Short-Term Holder Cost Basis Cluster

Glassnode’s latest cost-basis distribution analysis provides another important reference for Bitcoin’s current price structure. Its November 11 report identified a concentrated short-term holder supply band between $62,000 and $66,000, associated with coins acquired around the August market low.

Bitcoin is trading near its densest short-term holder cost-basis cluster, with concentrated supply between $63,000 and $65,000. Source: Glassnode via X

Glassnode described the distribution by stating that “the only dense block sits between $62K and $66K.” With Bitcoin trading near $65,000, its price has returned to this concentrated acquisition zone. The area could influence near-term trading as investors who purchased during that period reassess their positions.

The broader short-term holder picture remains less encouraging. Glassnode placed the average short-term holder cost basis at approximately $75,600, representing the average acquisition price for investors who purchased or last moved their Bitcoin within the previous 155 days. Its November 11 assessment found that only about 35% of short-term holder coins remained profitable.

This creates two separate market considerations. The $62,000–$66,000 distribution band identifies a concentrated area of recent acquisition activity, while the higher $75,600 average cost basis indicates that much of the broader short-term holder cohort remains underwater.

If Bitcoin maintains its position around the lower cluster, the area could provide a reference for near-term stabilization. However, a sustained decline below it would place more recent buyers into unrealized losses and could increase selling pressure if holders decide to exit their positions.

Bitcoin Apparent Demand Remains Deeply Negative

Despite the encouraging MVRV reading, Bitcoin’s demand indicators continue to present a significant concern. CryptoQuant analyst MorenoDV reported that BTC’s 30-day apparent demand stood near negative 247,700 BTC on November 15, while the one-year reading reached approximately negative 1.21 million BTC.

Bitcoin (BTC) price chart. Source: Brave New Coin

Apparent demand estimates the difference between newly issued Bitcoin and changes in the supply of coins that have remained inactive for more than a year. It helps assess whether new issuance is being absorbed by market participants or whether older supply is returning to circulation faster than new coins are being issued.

The persistent negative readings indicate that the market has not established a sustained accumulation trend. CryptoQuant also observed that demand failed to recover meaningfully during previous Bitcoin rebounds toward $80,000–$85,000, suggesting that those advances lacked confirmation from this on-chain indicator.

The analyst identified “the repeated failure of demand to recover” as the central concern. This distinction is important because a deeply negative apparent demand reading does not directly measure capital leaving the market or establish how many Bitcoins have been sold. Instead, it provides an estimate of changes in supply dynamics based on issuance and coin activity.

For a more convincing recovery, the 30-day reading would need to improve consistently and eventually return above zero. Stabilization in the annual measure would provide additional evidence that broader demand conditions are changing.

ETF Outflows Continue to Weigh on Bitcoin and Ethereum

Institutional investment flows provide another indication of the market’s current weakness. According to The Block’s November 14 reporting, U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded approximately $1.07 billion in net outflows during the week ending November 13.

The withdrawals marked the second consecutive week in which Bitcoin investment funds lost more than $1 billion. Over seven consecutive trading sessions, the funds recorded approximately $1.91 billion in net outflows, highlighting continued institutional caution during the latest price correction.

Ethereum (ETH) price chart. Source: Brave New Coin

Ethereum investment products experienced similar pressure. Spot ETH ETFs recorded approximately $398.9 million in weekly withdrawals, marking their seventh consecutive week of net outflows. The prolonged withdrawal period has weighed on Ethereum’s medium-term outlook despite its recent intraday recovery.

These figures suggest that the latest market bounce has not yet been accompanied by a meaningful reversal in institutional investment flows. A stabilization in ETF demand could help improve market conditions, although fund movements alone cannot determine the direction of future prices.

ETH and XRP Join the Intraday Crypto Market Recovery

Ethereum has shown some short-term strength alongside Bitcoin’s modest recovery. ETH climbed 1.04% to $1,838.34 after touching an intraday low near $1,809. The move also pushed the asset above immediate resistance around $1,827, suggesting that buyers are attempting to regain short-term control.

Oversold conditions have encouraged some buying activity following the recent decline. However, seven consecutive weeks of negative net flows from spot Ethereum ETFs remain a concern. The recovery therefore represents an improvement in intraday momentum rather than confirmation of a broader trend reversal.

XRP price chart. Source: Brave New Coin

Brave New Coin’s recent Ethereum price analysis also examines the $1,700 support region and the resistance levels ETH needs to reclaim to improve its technical structure.

XRP has followed a similar pattern, rising 1.04% to $0.9646 after recovering from an early session low near $0.9510. The rebound has brought the token closer to the psychologically important $1.00 level, although its broader technical structure remains bearish.

XRP continues to trade below its key moving averages, indicating that sellers retain control of the prevailing trend. Traders are watching the $0.895 support area, which could become important if another round of selling emerges. A sustained recovery above $1.00 would provide an initial indication of improving momentum, but further confirmation would be necessary to reverse the broader decline.

What Could Confirm a Bitcoin Market Bottom?

Bitcoin’s latest on-chain readings present a mixed picture. The MVRV ratio near 1.1 suggests that aggregate market valuation has moved into territory historically associated with bear-market bottoms. Glassnode’s cost-basis distribution also identifies a concentrated supply region around current prices.

However, negative apparent demand and continued ETF withdrawals show that market participation remains weak. The divergence between lower valuations and limited accumulation makes it difficult to establish whether Bitcoin has entered a durable recovery phase.

A sustained improvement in demand, a reduction in institutional withdrawals, and stronger price action around the $62,000–$66,000 region would provide additional evidence of stabilization. Reclaiming higher cost-basis levels would also help improve the position of recent investors.

For ETH and XRP, the immediate challenge remains maintaining their intraday gains and overcoming nearby resistance. Ethereum needs to build on its move above $1,827, while XRP must regain the $1.00 level and improve its position relative to key moving averages.

For now, the latest rebound offers some relief after a difficult trading period, but the underlying data remain mixed. Bitcoin’s MVRV reading raises the possibility of a developing market bottom, while weak demand and ETF withdrawals indicate that a sustained recovery has yet to receive broader confirmation.
